Questions

Before you ask.

Where do our files actually live?

In your own Google Drive, in the folders you point Deadreckoner at. We read and classify what's there — we never copy it into our own storage.

What happens if we cancel?

Your Drive is unaffected either way, since we never held the files. Revoking Drive access from your Google account settings cuts off our access immediately.

How does the AI quality gate decide what passes?

It checks each asset against minimum technical standards for its category — resolution, format, and production requirements — before it's eligible for approval.

Can we export our brand guidelines?

Yes — approved assets, rules, and identity decisions resolve into a downloadable PDF brand book whenever you need it.

Do you support more than one brand per account?

Multi-brand support is part of the Enterprise plan. Get in touch if that's your situation.
